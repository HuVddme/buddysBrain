# Buddy’s Brain

**Learn from your course materials—and see the sources behind each answer.**

Buddy’s Brain helps students turn notes, documents, articles, and videos into a searchable study library. Ask a question, read Buddy’s explanation, and open the supporting passages to check where the answer came from.

[Try Buddy’s Brain](https://buddysbrain.vercel.app/) · [Explore the demo](https://buddysbrain.vercel.app/ask) · [Ask a live question](https://buddysbrain.vercel.app/ask?mode=live)

## What you can do

- **Ask about your material.** Type a question or dictate it in a supported browser, then watch the answer appear as Buddy responds.
- **Check the sources.** Open citation chips to read the original passages behind an answer. Each answer keeps its own sources, so earlier conversations remain easy to follow.
- **Grow the library.** Add PDFs, Word (.docx) documents, text notes, images, articles, or YouTube links. Document uploads show their progress and let you retry recoverable failures. Repeated uploads of the same document are recognized.
- **Try it without setup.** Explore three sample lessons and six prepared answers, with supporting passages and downloadable notes. The demo needs no account or API key and makes no live AI requests.
- **Pick up where you left off.** Demo lesson progress stays in your browser, with a shortcut to your last answer.
- **Study comfortably.** Use the site on desktop or mobile, switch to dark mode, and navigate with a keyboard. Completion sounds are optional and off by default; motion follows your device’s accessibility preference.

## Start exploring

1. **Explore demo** to try sample lessons on limits and continuity, binary heaps, and database normalization. Choose a question to see its saved answer and supporting notes.
2. **Ask live** to ask your own question about the connected library. Open the sources under the answer to read the evidence for yourself.
3. **Add a resource** to share useful course material. For documents, follow the processing status and return when it is ready. New material may take a moment to appear in search.

## A few things to know

- **This is a shared library.** There are no separate student accounts or private document collections. Only add material you have permission to share, and leave out confidential or personal information.
- **Answers still need checking.** AI can make mistakes. Answers without supporting library material are labeled as general knowledge; Buddy does not perform a live web search.
- **The demo and live library are separate.** Demo answers are saved examples based on original sample notes, not answers generated from your uploads.
- **Live usage is limited.** Shared and per-visitor allowances help keep the public site available. If AI generation fails after finding sources, you can still read those sources.
- **Voice input depends on your browser.** It has been verified in Chrome. Dia currently fails to reach its speech-recognition service; typing remains available.
- **Uploads have a size limit.** The live site accepts files up to 4 MB and explains unsupported files or processing failures.

## About this version

Buddy’s Brain began as a team project at Bison Bytes 2025. This independently deployed version continues that work with source-backed answers, more reliable document uploads, clearer recovery from failures, and a consistent study interface.

The project’s original Git history and contributor credits are preserved from [the original team repository](https://github.com/RobelK1738/Buddys-Brain).
